# Seven Years' War

## Overview

The **Seven Years' War** (1756–1763) was a global conflict fought between Britain and France (and their respective allies) across Europe, the Americas, Africa, and Asia. In North America, it is known as the **[[French and Indian War]]**. Britain's decisive victory reshaped the world map and set the stage for the [[American Revolution]].

## Global Scope

The war was fought on every inhabited continent, making it arguably the first true "world war." The main combatants were Britain (allied with Prussia) versus France (allied with Austria, Russia, and Spain).

## North American Theater

In the Americas ([[French and Indian War]]), Britain defeated France and its Native American allies, winning all of French Canada and lands east of the Mississippi River. Spain, entering the war late, surrendered Florida to Britain.

## Consequences

Britain emerged as the dominant global power but faced enormous war debts. To pay for the war (and future colonial defense), Britain began taxing the colonies — the **[[Stamp Act]]**, **[[Townshend Acts]]**, and other measures that led directly to colonial resistance and the [[American Revolution]].
