# French Republic

## Overview

The **French Republic** refers to the republican government that replaced the French monarchy after the **[[French Revolution]]** beginning in 1792. The First French Republic (1792–1804) was characterized by the radical **Reign of Terror**, the rise of more moderate governments, and ultimately **[[Napoleon Bonaparte]]'s** coup in 1799, which established the Consulate and eventually the First French Empire.

## Impact on America

The French Republic deeply divided American politics. **[[Thomas Jefferson]]** and **[[Democratic-Republican Party|Democratic-Republicans]]** saw it as the extension of revolutionary ideals to Europe. **[[Alexander Hamilton]]** and **[[Federalist Party|Federalists]]** were horrified by its violent excesses.

## Quasi-War

The French Republic's Directory government — annoyed that the U.S. had signed **[[Jay's Treaty]]** with Britain without consulting France — began seizing American merchant ships. This led to the **[[Quasi-War]]** (1798–1800), an undeclared naval conflict with the U.S.

## Napoleon's Rise

The instability of the French Republic led to Napoleon Bonaparte's coup in November 1799 (18 Brumaire), establishing the Consulate. Napoleon's subsequent decisions — including selling the **[[Louisiana Territory]]** to the United States — transformed American history.
