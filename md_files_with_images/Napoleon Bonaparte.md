**Napoleon Bonaparte** (1769–1821) was the French military genius and emperor whose ambitions and defeats profoundly shaped American history. His decision to sell the **[[Louisiana Territory]]** to the United States in 1803 doubled the size of the young nation.

---

## The Louisiana Sale

> [!info] Why Napoleon Sold
> Napoleon had planned a French empire in the Americas centered on Haiti, but the **Haitian Revolution** (1791–1804) destroyed his army. Facing renewed war with Britain and unable to defend Louisiana, he offered the entire territory to the U.S. — surprising American diplomats who had only gone to buy New Orleans. The sale prevented Britain from seizing Louisiana and gave Napoleon funds for his European wars.

---

## Impact on American Foreign Policy

> [!info] Napoleon's Wars and America
> Napoleon's wars with Britain created constant problems for the neutral United States:
> - Both France and Britain seized American ships → led to the **[[Quasi-War]]** with France
> - British impressment and trade restrictions → led to the **[[Embargo Act]]** (1807)
> - Continued British interference → led to the **[[War of 1812]]**
