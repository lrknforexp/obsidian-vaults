# Napoleon Bonaparte

> [!info] Quick Facts
> **Born:** August 15, 1769 — Ajaccio, Corsica
> **Died:** May 5, 1821 — Saint Helena (British exile)
> **Role:** French military general; First Consul (1799–1804); Emperor of the French (1804–1814, 1815)
> **Relevance to APUSH:** Sold the [[Louisiana Purchase|Louisiana Territory]] to the United States (1803); caused the [[Quasi-War]] through French seizures of American ships

---

**Napoleon Bonaparte** was the French military genius and emperor who dominated Europe from 1799 to 1815. For American history, he matters primarily because:
1. The **[[Quasi-War]]** — French seizures of American ships during his rise to power pushed the U.S. and France to the brink of war
2. The **[[Louisiana Purchase]]** — Napoleon sold the Louisiana Territory to the United States in 1803, doubling the nation's size

---

## Rise to Power

Napoleon rose from relative obscurity as a Corsican artillery officer to become the most powerful figure in Europe through brilliant military campaigns during the French Revolutionary Wars (1792–1802). In 1799, he seized power in a coup, becoming First Consul of France, and in 1804 crowned himself Emperor.

---

## American Relevance: The Quasi-War

French seizures of American merchant ships — retaliation for **[[Jay's Treaty]]** — fueled the **[[Quasi-War]]** (1798–1800) between the U.S. and France. Napoleon, seizing power in France in 1799, was more interested in European dominance than fighting the Americans, and quickly concluded the **Convention of 1800** (Treaty of Mortefontaine) normalizing U.S.-French relations.

---

## The Louisiana Purchase (1803)

> [!info] Why Napoleon Sold
> Napoleon had grand plans for a French empire in the Americas, centered on the Caribbean colony of **Saint-Domingue** (Haiti). The **Haitian Revolution** — a massive slave uprising — destroyed the French army sent to suppress it. Without Haiti, the Louisiana Territory was strategically useless to Napoleon.
>
> Facing renewed war with Britain and needing money, Napoleon offered the entire Louisiana Territory — roughly 828,000 square miles — to the United States for **$15 million**. The sale was completed on April 30, 1803.

This decision doubled the size of the United States and set it on the path to becoming a continental power.

---

## Defeat and Exile

Napoleon's empire began collapsing after his disastrous invasion of Russia (1812). He was defeated by a European coalition, abdicated in 1814, returned briefly in 1815 (**the Hundred Days**), was defeated at Waterloo (June 18, 1815), and spent his final years in British exile on the island of Saint Helena, where he died in 1821.
