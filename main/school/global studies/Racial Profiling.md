## 📖 Key Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Racial Profiling** | Targeting or suspecting a person based on their race — assuming group behavior rather than judging the individual |
| **Stereotype** | A generalization made about an entire group of people |
| **Race** | A group that shares common physical traits and social qualities |
| **Ethnicity** | Cultural factors including nationality, regional culture, ancestry, and language |

> 💡 **Race vs. Ethnicity:** Race = physical/social traits. Ethnicity = cultural background & heritage.

---

## 🇺🇸 Racial Profiling in the United States

- In the US, racial profiling is closely tied to **law enforcement** and the treatment of **nonwhite people**
- Profiling looks **different depending on the racial or ethnic group** being targeted

### How Profiling Shows Up by Group

| Group | Common Profiling Assumption |
|-------|-----------------------------|
| Black Americans | Suspected of drug-related crimes |
| Latino/Hispanic people | Suspected of being undocumented immigrants |
| Arabs & South Asians | Assumed to be Muslim and potentially a terrorist sympathizer |

---

## ⚖️ Laws & Policies That Have Enabled Profiling

| Policy | What It Did |
|--------|-------------|
| **Stop and Frisk** | Allowed police to stop and search individuals based on suspicion |
| **Immigration Act of 1924** | Restricted immigration based on national origin and race |
| **Internment** | Forced relocation/imprisonment of Japanese Americans during WWII |
| **Trump's Travel Ban** | Restricted entry to the US from several majority-Muslim countries |

## ⚡ Quick Recap

```
Stereotype (generalization about a group)
        +
Race / Ethnicity (visible identity)
        ↓
Racial Profiling (targeting an individual based on group assumptions)
```

Profiling ≠ judging the **individual** — it's wrongly applying **group assumptions** to a person.
