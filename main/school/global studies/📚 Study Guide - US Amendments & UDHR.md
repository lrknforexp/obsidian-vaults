## 🇺🇸 US Constitutional Amendments

### The Bill of Rights (Amendments 1–10)

| # | Name | What It Does |
|---|------|--------------|
| 1st | Freedom of Religion, Speech, Press, Assembly, Petition | Protects basic civil liberties |
| 2nd | Right to Bear Arms | Allows citizens to own weapons |
| 3rd | Quartering of Soldiers | No forced housing of soldiers |
| 4th | Search and Seizure | Protects against unreasonable searches |
| 5th | Rights of the Accused | Due process, no self-incrimination, no double jeopardy |
| 6th | Right to Trial | Speedy and fair criminal trial |
| 7th | Civil Trial by Jury | Jury required in civil cases |
| 8th | Cruel and Unusual Punishment | Limits excessive punishment |
| 9th | Rights Retained by the People | Protects rights not listed in the Constitution |
| 10th | Powers of States | Powers not given to federal gov't go to states |

---

### Later Amendments (11–27)

| # | Name | What It Does |
|---|------|--------------|
| 11th | State Sovereign Immunity | Limits ability to sue states |
| 12th | Election of President & VP | Fixes the presidential election process |
| 13th | Abolition of Slavery | Ends slavery in the US |
| 14th | Citizenship & Equal Protection | Guarantees rights to all citizens |
| 15th | Voting Rights (Race) | Cannot deny vote based on race |
| 16th | Income Tax | Allows federal income tax |
| 17th | Direct Election of Senators | Voters (not state legislatures) elect senators |
| 18th | Prohibition | Bans alcohol |
| 19th | Women's Suffrage | Women can vote |
| 20th | Terms of Office | Sets start/end dates for presidential and congressional terms |
| 21st | Repeal of Prohibition | Ends the alcohol ban (overturns 18th) |
| 22nd | Presidential Term Limits | Maximum of two terms as president |
| 23rd | D.C. Electoral Votes | Washington D.C. gets electoral votes |
| 24th | Poll Tax Ban | Cannot charge a tax to vote |
| 25th | Presidential Succession | Rules for replacing the president |
| 26th | Voting Age | Voting age set to 18 |
| 27th | Congressional Pay | Delays congressional pay raises until after next election |

---

### 🔑 Key Groupings to Remember

- **Civil Liberties:** 1st, 4th, 5th, 6th, 8th
- **Reconstruction Era (post-Civil War):** 13th, 14th, 15th
- **Progressive Era:** 16th, 17th, 18th, 19th
- **Modern Era:** 22nd–27th
- **Repealed & Replaced:** 18th → 21st

## 🌍 Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR)

### Articles 1–10: Fundamental Rights

| # | Right | Summary |
|---|-------|---------|
| 1 | Born Free & Equal | All humans have dignity and rights |
| 2 | No Discrimination | Rights apply regardless of race, gender, religion, origin, etc. |
| 3 | Right to Life | You have the right to live and be autonomous |
| 4 | No Slavery | Slavery and slave trade are illegal |
| 5 | No Torture | No degrading or inhumane treatment |
| 6 | Right to Use Law | You are a person before the law everywhere |
| 7 | Equal Before the Law | Equal protection, free from discrimination |
| 8 | Fair Treatment by Court | You can get legal help when your rights are contested |
| 9 | No Unfair Detainment | No random arrest or exile |
| 10 | Right to Trial | Fair and public trial |

---

### Articles 11–20: Personal Freedoms

| # | Right | Summary |
|---|-------|---------|
| 11 | Innocent Until Proven Guilty | Presumed innocent until proven guilty at trial |
| 12 | Right to Privacy | No interference with privacy, family, or reputation |
| 13 | Freedom of Movement | Can move within and between countries |
| 14 | Right to Asylum | Can flee to another country to avoid persecution (non-political crimes excluded) |
| 15 | Right to Nationality | Everyone has a nationality and can change it |
| 16 | Right to Marry & Family | Adults can marry by mutual consent and form a family |
| 17 | Right to Own Property | The right to own things |
| 18 | Freedom of Thought & Religion | Can follow any religion or belief |
| 19 | Freedom of Opinion & Expression | Can hold and express opinions freely |
| 20 | Right to Assemble | Can gather and associate with others |

---

### Articles 21–30: Social & Economic Rights

| # | Right | Summary |
|---|-------|---------|
| 21 | Right to Democracy | Can participate in your country's government |
| 22 | Right to Social Security | Society should help you access available benefits |
| 23 | Right to Work | Can choose work, have decent conditions, and receive equal pay |
| 24 | Right to Rest & Holiday | Time off from work and holiday pay |
| 25 | Right to Social Services | Right to food, shelter, clothing, and healthcare |
| 26 | Right to Education | Free elementary education; higher education available to all |
| 27 | Right to Culture & Art | Can participate in community culture; creations are protected |
| 28 | Freedom Around the World | Everyone is entitled to these rights globally |
| 29 | Subject to Law | Law exists to guarantee and protect these rights |
| 30 | Rights Cannot Be Taken Away | No person or group can act to strip others of human rights |

---

### 🔑 Key UDHR Groupings

- **Personal Security:** Articles 3–5, 9, 11
- **Legal Rights:** Articles 6–8, 10, 12
- **Civil Liberties:** Articles 13–20
- **Political Rights:** Articles 21, 28–30
- **Economic & Social Rights:** Articles 22–27
