**Thomas Jefferson** (1743–1826) was the **3rd President of the United States**, the primary author of the **[[Declaration of Independence]]**, and co-founder of the **[[Democratic-Republican Party]]**. He articulated the founding ideals of American democracy while living in profound contradiction with them as an enslaver.

---

## Key Beliefs

> [!info] Jefferson's Vision for America
> Jefferson believed in:
> - An **agrarian republic** of independent farmers as the foundation of democracy
> - **Strict construction** of the Constitution — only explicitly granted powers are legitimate
> - **States' rights** over federal power
> - **Separation of church and state**
> - Natural rights and human equality — in theory

---

## Major Actions as President

> [!info] Jefferson's Presidency (1801–1809)
> - **[[Louisiana Purchase]]** (1803) — doubled U.S. territory; strained his strict constructionist principles
> - **[[Lewis and Clark Expedition]]** — commissioned to explore the new territory
> - **[[Embargo Act]]** (1807) — attempted economic coercion of Britain and France; backfired badly
> - **[[Marbury v. Madison]]** — his refusal to deliver Marbury's commission triggered the landmark case

---

## Contradictions

> [!info] "All Men Are Created Equal"
> Jefferson wrote that "all men are created equal" yet enslaved over 600 people. His relationship with enslaved woman **[[Sally Hemings]]** produced six children. This fundamental contradiction between Jefferson's ideals and actions remains central to understanding American history.
