# Civil War

> [!info] Civil War
> **Overview**
>
> **Topic**
> American Civil War — Union vs. Confederacy
>
> **Dates**
> 1861–1865
>
> **Cause**
> Slavery and the question of secession

> [!info] Quick Facts
> **Dates:** 1861–1865
> **Combatants:** Union (North) vs. Confederacy (11 Southern states)
> **Deaths:** ~620,000–750,000 soldiers
> **Ended:** Lee surrendered to Grant at Appomattox, April 9, 1865
> **Result:** Union preserved; slavery abolished

---

The **American Civil War** (1861–1865) was the deadliest conflict in American history, fought between the **Union** (Northern states) and the **Confederacy** (eleven Southern states that seceded). At its core, the war was fought over **slavery** and the question of whether states could leave the Union.

---

## Causes

> [!info] Why the War Happened
> Decades of sectional conflict over slavery's expansion culminated when **Abraham Lincoln** was elected president in 1860 without a single Southern electoral vote. Southern states feared Lincoln would restrict slavery. South Carolina seceded in December 1860, followed by ten other states forming the **Confederate States of America**.

## Key Events

| Event | Date | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| Attack on Fort Sumter | April 1861 | War begins |
| Battle of Antietam | September 1862 | Bloodiest single day; opens door for Emancipation Proclamation |
| Emancipation Proclamation | January 1, 1863 | Frees enslaved people in Confederate states |
| Battles of Gettysburg & Vicksburg | July 1863 | Major turning point; Union controls Mississippi |
| Sherman's March to the Sea | 1864 | Total war devastates the South |
| Appomattox | April 9, 1865 | Lee surrenders to Grant |

---

## Outcome

> [!info] Results of the War
> Confederate General **Robert E. Lee** surrendered to **[[Ulysses S. Grant]]** at Appomattox Court House on April 9, 1865. The war killed approximately **620,000–750,000** soldiers, ended slavery in America (confirmed by the **[[14th Amendment]]**), and preserved the Union.
