# Manifest Destiny

> [!info] Manifest Destiny
> **Overview**
>
> **Topic**
> 19th-century belief in American continental expansion
>
> **Era**
> 1840s–1890s (peak: 1840s–1860s)
>
> **Coined by**
> Journalist John O'Sullivan, 1845

> [!info] Quick Facts
> **Term coined:** 1845 by John O'Sullivan
> **Core belief:** The U.S. was divinely destined to stretch from Atlantic to Pacific
> **Key acquisitions enabled:** Texas, Oregon, California, Southwest
> **Dark side:** Displacement and destruction of Native American peoples

---

**Manifest Destiny** was the 19th-century belief that the United States was **divinely ordained** to expand across the North American continent from the Atlantic to the Pacific Ocean. It provided the ideological justification for westward expansion, territorial acquisitions, and the displacement of Native Americans and Mexicans.

---

## Origins and Meaning

> [!info] What "Manifest Destiny" Meant
> Journalist John O'Sullivan coined the phrase in 1845, arguing it was America's "manifest destiny to overspread the continent." The idea combined:
> - **Religious conviction** — God had chosen Americans to spread democracy and Christianity
> - **Racial ideology** — Anglo-Saxon civilization was superior and destined to dominate
> - **Democratic mission** — American free institutions should spread across the continent

## Consequences for Native Americans

> [!info] The Human Cost
> Manifest Destiny was catastrophic for Native Americans. The **[[Indian Removal Act]]** forced eastern tribes west. As settlers moved further west, wars, disease, and broken treaties destroyed Native nations across the continent. The ideology provided moral cover for what amounted to ethnic cleansing.
