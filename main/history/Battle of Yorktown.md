The **Battle of Yorktown** (September–October 1781) was the last major military engagement of the [[American Revolution]] and resulted in the surrender of British General **[[Cornwallis]]** to American and French forces. It effectively ended the war and secured American independence.

---

## How Cornwallis Got Trapped

> [!info] The Strategic Situation
> Cornwallis marched his army into Virginia and established a base at Yorktown on the Chesapeake Bay — a fatal strategic error. General **[[George Washington]]** and French General Rochambeau marched their combined army south from New York. Simultaneously, the French fleet under Admiral de Grasse defeated the British navy at the **Battle of the Chesapeake**, cutting off Cornwallis's escape by sea.

---

## The Siege

The allied forces besieged Yorktown for three weeks, using artillery to systematically destroy British defensive positions. On **October 19, 1781**, Cornwallis surrendered his army of roughly 8,000 soldiers. Cornwallis himself claimed illness, sending a subordinate to hand over his sword.

---

## Aftermath

> [!info] Road to Peace
> Yorktown led directly to peace negotiations. The **[[Treaty of Paris (1783)]]** formally ended the war in 1783, with Britain recognizing American independence and ceding vast territories east of the Mississippi River to the new nation.
