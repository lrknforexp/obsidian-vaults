**Immigration** - The action of coming to live permanently in a foreign country
**Emigration** - The action of leaving a country to settle permanently in another.
**Migrant** - A person who moves from one place to another especially in order to find work or better living conditions.

### **Migration**
**Push factors** - reasons people leave a place.
**Pull factors** - reasons people come to a place.
Reasons why people leave home is because of war, poverty, disasters, famine and crime.
Reasons why people go to the area they choose is because it offers jobs, safety, good environment and political safety.

### types of migration
**Economic migration** - moving to find work or follow a particular career path.
**Social migration** - moving somewhere for a better quality of life or to be closer to family.
**Political migration** - moving to escape political persecution or war.
**Environmental migration** - migration from natural disaster.
### Rights of migrants and violations they face
- Due to the UDHR migrants have rights like every other human anywhere in the world.
- And in the U.S. they are protected by the constitution.
- Violations that migrants go through are, denial of civil and political rights, Arbitary detention, torture, lack of due process, rights to health, housing and education.
- Discriminatory practices are linked to prejudice and xenophobia
### Pros
- For the host country it reduces a labor shortage and diversifies the culture.
- For the home country, the migrants can return with new skills, money can come in, less stress on resources.
## Cons
- The host country faces overcrowding and increase in cost of healthcare and education.
- for the home country there is brain drain, shrinking workforce and gender imbalance.
