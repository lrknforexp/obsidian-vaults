---

### Definition

**Grief** – the emotional response to experiencing a loss (e.g., the death of a dog or a family member).

---

### Types of Loss

Any loss can cause grief, including:

- Loss of friendship
- Loss of health
- Loss of job
- Death of a loved one
- Loss of a pet
- Divorce or relationship breakup
- Loss of financial stability

---

### What Is Loss?

- An undesired change or removal of a valued object, person, or situation.

**Elisabeth Kübler‑Ross**

- Swiss psychiatrist; pioneer of near‑death studies.
- Author of _On Death and Dying_, which records personal conversations between patients and doctors.
- Began studying dying patients after noticing the lack of academic knowledge on the topic.

---

### The 5 Stages of Grief (Kübler‑Ross Model)

|Stage|Typical Experiences / Behaviors|
|---|---|
|**1. Denial**|• Disbelief, shock, refusal to accept the loss  <br>• Minimizing or denying the situation  <br>• “This can’t be happening.”|
|**2. Anger**|• Desire to fight back or get even  <br>• Blaming others – “Who is to blame?”  <br>• Questions like “Why is this happening to me?”  <br>• Support the person; anger is a normal reaction.|
|**3. Bargaining**|• Attempts to postpone sadness (e.g., “If I … then …”)|
|**4. Depression**|• Overwhelming helplessness, crying, screaming|
|**5. Acceptance**|• Calm, retrospective view  <br>• Moving on, exploring options, new plans in place  <br>• “It is going to be okay.”|

### Matching Activity (Stage ↔ Description)

- **Stage One** – Denial
- **Stage Two** – Anger, cursing
- **Stage Three** – Bargaining, speaking to God and begging
- **Stage Four** – Depression, crying, and screaming
- **Stage Five** – Acceptance, thinking something good waits for him in heaven
