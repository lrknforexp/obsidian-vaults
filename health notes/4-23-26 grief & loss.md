grief is when you experience a loss. for example: your dog dying or a family member dying you might feel sad and grieve.

types of loss 
any loss can cause grief, including
loss of friendship, loss of health, loss of job, death of loved one, loss of pet, divorce, or relationship breakup, loss of financial stability.

What is loss?
- An undesired change or removal of a valued object, person, or situation.
- Elisabeth Kubler-Ross Dr. Kubler-Ross was a Swiss psychiatrist. she pioneered the field of near death studies and wrote “on death and dying”. in her book she wrote several personal conversations between patient and doctor. she began studying those that were going to die after realizing that the universities had virtually no knowledge on the topic
- Denial, anger, bargaining, depression, acceptance.

5 stages of grief
1. Denial 
- May experience DISBELIEF
- May experience SHOCK
- May refuse to accept the loss
- May minimize or deny the situation
- May be trying to avoid
- “This can’t be happening.”

2. Anger
- May feel like wanting to fight back
- May feel the need to get even
- May feel the need to blame others “who is to blame?”
- “why is this happening to me?” “why me”
- Support friend & let them know its ok.
- They have the right to be angry.
- Anger is normal, natural reaction.

3. bargaining
- Attempt to postpone your sadness

4. Depression
- may be overwhelming, helplessness

5. acceptance
- calm retrospective view
- moving on
- exploring options
- new plans in place
- “it is going to be ok”
